This white paper explores the governance of **agentic AI systems**, which are defined by their ability to pursue complex goals with minimal human oversight.

The authors establish a framework for safety and accountability, identifying the distinct responsibilities of model developers, system deployers, and end users.

To mitigate risks such as autonomous errors or misuse, the text proposes seven core practices, including constraining action-spaces, ensuring activity legibility, and maintaining interruptibility.

Beyond individual technical failures, the sources examine broader societal challenges like labor displacement, competitive adoption races, and correlated systemic failures.

Ultimately, the research seeks to catalyze a global dialogue on creating baseline best practices to ensure these high-impact technologies remain under human control.
