This survey explores the evolution of **Agentic AI**, moving from passive tools toward autonomous systems capable of independent planning and collaboration.

The authors introduce a dual-paradigm framework to distinguish between the symbolic lineage, which uses deterministic logic, and the neural lineage, which relies on generative models and stochastic orchestration.

By reviewing 90 studies, the paper clarifies how these distinct architectures are applied across sectors like healthcare, finance, and robotics.

The analysis addresses the problem of conceptual retrofitting, where modern AI is incorrectly described using outdated symbolic theories.

Ultimately, the research provides a strategic roadmap advocating for the integration of both paradigms to develop reliable, adaptive, and ethically governed intelligent systems.
