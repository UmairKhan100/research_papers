This research paper introduces the **Transformer**, a pioneering network architecture designed for sequence transduction that abandons traditional recurrent and convolutional structures.

By relying entirely on self-attention mechanisms, the model can process data in parallel, leading to significantly faster training times and improved computational efficiency.

The authors demonstrate that this approach achieves state-of-the-art results in English-to-German and English-to-French translation tasks.

Beyond translation, the study proves the generalization capabilities of the Transformer by successfully applying it to English constituency parsing.

Ultimately, the work establishes a new standard for neural machine translation by prioritizing global dependencies and attention-based learning.
