This 2022 research paper provides a comprehensive review of **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**, tracing its historical evolution from early machine translation to modern deep learning architectures.

The authors categorize the field into **Natural Language Understanding (NLU)** and **Natural Language Generation (NLG)**, detailing various linguistic levels such as phonology, morphology, and semantics.

It highlights significant state-of-the-art applications, including spam filtering, sentimental analysis, and medical information extraction, while showcasing real-world projects like chatbots and translation earbuds.

Furthermore, the text identifies essential datasets and evaluation metrics like **BLEU** and **GLUE** that are used to measure algorithmic performance.

Finally, the study addresses persistent challenges in the field, such as sarcasm, ambiguity, and the complexities of regional languages.
